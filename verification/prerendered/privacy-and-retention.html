Storage and retention

Clear control over captured images.

DICOM Camera keeps images captured in the application within the app. Export to the Photo Album is an explicit action, and local retention can follow the result of sending images to the archive.

Captures remain in the application

Capturing an image in DICOM Camera does not automatically add it to the Photo Album. Use an explicit export when you need a copy there.

Import existing photographs

Already captured images can be imported from the Photo Album for patient association and upload. Import and export are separate actions.

Automatic deletion after successful send

Auto-delete after send is enabled by default. When no Storage Commitment gate is configured, successful send is the condition for automatic deletion. Failed sends do not trigger deletion.

Wait for Storage Commitment when required

Enable Storage Commitment gating to wait for a positive commitment result from the archive before deleting the relevant local objects. While commitment is pending, or if it fails, those objects remain retained.

Fit retention to the deployment

Review the intended behaviour with your clinical and imaging IT teams, including whether the archive provides Storage Commitment and how your workflow handles unsuccessful transfers.
